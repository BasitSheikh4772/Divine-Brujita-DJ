  • My name is DJ, and I am a devoted follower of Santísima Madre. Thank you for taking the time to visit my website and learn a little about me.

    Helping others has always been the purpose of my life. Whether through my years in healthcare, my spiritual practice, or the handmade products I create through Divine Creations, my passion has always been the same to bring comfort, guidance, hope, and support to those who need it most.

    I believe every person has a unique journey, and I feel truly honored whenever someone trusts me to be a small part of theirs.

  • I have seen and heard spirits for as long as I can remember.

    As a child, these experiences were frightening. I struggled with nightmares, sleep paralysis, and encounters I couldn't explain. I turned to churches and priests for help, hoping someone would understand what I was experiencing. Instead, I was often told to simply pray more or that perhaps I had done something wrong. As an eight-year-old little girl, those words left me feeling confused and alone.

    As I grew older, those experiences never disappeared.

    Instead of continuing to fear them, I chose to learn. Over time, I developed a deeper understanding of my gifts and learned how to protect myself, work with spiritual energy, and help others do the same.

    What once felt like a burden eventually became my life's calling.

  • I have been happily married for over 15 years and am incredibly blessed with four amazing children.

    My family is originally from Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico, where faith, family, and tradition have always been deeply valued. My spiritual path has been shaped by my ancestry, my personal experiences, and the lessons passed down through generations.

    Family means everything to me, and it reminds me every day why I approach each client with kindness, compassion, and respect

  • Before dedicating myself fully to my spiritual practice, I spent nearly 20 years working in healthcare, including caring for hospice patients and serving for 10 years in the Emergency Department at Kaiser Permanente.

    Those years changed my life forever.

    I stood beside people during some of the most difficult moments of their lives. I witnessed loss, hope, strength, and love in ways that cannot be described.

    Those experiences taught me compassion, patience, humility, and the importance of treating every person with dignity.

    Eventually, I realized my calling extended beyond physical care. I felt called to help people spiritually to provide guidance, prayer, comfort, and support before life's burdens became too heavy to carry alone.

    That realization led me to dedicate myself completely to the work I do today.

  • Every spiritual session I perform is approached with sincerity, prayer, and respect.

    I do not believe in treating every situation the same because every person has a unique story. Before recommending any service, I take the time to understand your concerns and what may best support your journey.

    My goal is never to create fear.

    My goal is to help you find clarity, remove obstacles, strengthen your spiritual foundation, and move forward with greater peace and confidence.

  • Outside of my spiritual work, I also own Divine Creations, follow me on Instagram @ Divine_creations_by_dj where I handcraft products that combine creativity, self-care, and tradition. Every product is made by hand with care and attention to detail.

    Some of my handmade creations include:

    • Handmade soaps

    • Hair oils

    • Hair sprays

    • Protection sprays

    • Pheromone sprays

    • Black Witches Salt

    • Botanical bath tea bags

    • Crystal jewelry

    • Spiritual accessories

    …and many other unique creations.

    Creating with my hands has always brought me joy. Whether I'm preparing a spiritual session or making a handcrafted product, I pour the same love, care, and dedication into everything I do. You can placing your order here DIVINE CREATIONS

  • Everything I do is guided by a few simple beliefs.

    • Faith should bring peace, not fear.

    • Free will deserves to be respected.

    • Spiritual work should be approached with honesty and integrity.

    • Healing begins with self-awareness.

    • Divine timing cannot be forced.

    • Every client deserves kindness, compassion, and respect.

    These values guide every session, every conversation, and every recommendation I make.

  • One of the most important parts of my practice is maintaining strong personal and spiritual boundaries.

    For that reason, there are certain requests I will respectfully decline.

    I will never work to separate a marriage because I believe in honoring the sacred commitment of marriage.

    I also will not perform work that goes against my personal values or spiritual convictions.

    Integrity will always come before profit.

  • If you've taken the time to read my story, thank you.

    Whether you're here because you've been devoted to Santísima Madre for years or you're simply searching for guidance, I truly appreciate your visit.

    It is an honor to do the work that I do, and I never take your trust for granted.

    If you choose to work with me, know that every session is performed with faith, compassion, discretion, and genuine care.

    From my family to yours, I wish you peace, strength, abundance, and many blessings.

    With love,

    DJ 🤍
